Box

Opacity 0 hides the box whatever its colour. Size is the padding around the words, as a share of the type size.

Shadow

Distance 0 turns the shadow off. 45° puts it down and to the right, where a shadow normally falls; 225° puts it up and to the left.

Active word

Box highlight and Scale + box show one word at a time, each in its own box. Bar behind the whole caption keeps every word on one bar and marks the active word by colour: the news lower-third look.

Play a sound

Drag while a sound plays — level is judged by ear, not by number. −8 dB sits under a voice without disappearing.

Sounds

Pick up to four. They play in the order you picked them and then start again, so two sounds alternate. Play them first — the volume you set here is the volume you hear.

Negative nudge starts the sound before the word, which is usually what you want: the ear places a sound by its attack.

This build carries no sounds.

Throw an emoji

Emoji

Pick up to four. They are thrown in the order you picked them and then start again, so two emoji alternate.

Wider than the sounds' gap on purpose: a noise every third of a second is a rhythm, a picture every third of a second is a mess.

This build carries no emoji.
